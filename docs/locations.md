Karst Topography

- Yangshuo, China: Sheer limestone pillars and river valleys.
- Tsingy de Bemaraha, Madagascar: A dense "forest" of jagged, knife-edged limestone needles.

Slot Canyons & Gorges
- Grand Canyon, USA: Massive, mile-deep, steep-sided canyon carved by the Colorado River.
- Zion Narrows, USA: Extremely deep, narrow canyon with sheer vertical walls.
- Verdon Gorge, France: A plunging river canyon with sharp cliffs dropping up to 700 meters.

Fjords
- Milford Sound, New Zealand: Steep glacial valleys crashing directly into the ocean.
- Geirangerfjord, Norway: High-walled, twisting maritime fjords with tight turns.

Volcanic Calderas
- Mount Fuji, Japan: A 3,776-meter active stratovolcano and the highest mountain in Japan, famous worldwide for its graceful, symmetrical, snow-capped cone shape.
- Mount Aso, Japan: A massive volcanic caldera containing a central active cone and sharp outer rim.
- Ngorongoro Crater, Tanzania: A deep, unbroken 600-meter vertical caldera wall enclosing a flat interior.

Archipelagos
- Reine, Lofoten, Norway: Sharp, dramatic granite peaks forming a tight coastal island chain.
- Wayag, Raja Ampat, Indonesia: A chaotic maze of steep, bulbous karst islands dotting a shallow ocean.
